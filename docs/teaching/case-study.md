# Case card: published SLN360 study and separate teaching examples

## The real published study

The 2022 phase 1 SLN360 report is the real drug-development anchor. Use this compact card rather than reproducing the journal article. [Nissen et al., JAMA, 2022](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/fullarticle/2790912)

| Item | Published study |
| --- | --- |
| Question | Safety/tolerability and Lp(a) response after a single dose |
| Participants | 32 adults with elevated Lp(a), without known clinically overt cardiovascular disease |
| Design | Randomized ascending-dose cohorts; four active doses and pooled placebo |
| Group sizes | Six at each active dose; eight placebo participants overall |
| Primary outcome | Safety and tolerability |
| Other measurements | Lp(a) changes, with follow-up to 150 days |

The report found dose-related Lp(a) lowering and supports further investigation. A biomarker response in a small early study does not establish fewer cardiovascular events, universal safety, or regulatory approval. Lp(a) and LDL cholesterol are not interchangeable endpoints. [Published interpretation](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/fullarticle/2790912)

For class, open the article's baseline table, adverse-event table, and Lp(a) figure. Explain what each communicates and what question it leaves open. Do not recast the actual study as a generic two-arm confirmatory trial. The article's protocol and statistical-analysis-plan supplements are optional instructor references.

## Invented drug-data demonstration

**EDU-LPA-001** is a fictional two-arm, 24-participant teaching study with placebo and “Investigational A,” four visits, and an Lp(a) laboratory measurement. It is deliberately smaller and structurally different from SLN360. Its results are invented; it is not a simulation calibrated to the published trial, an estimate of SLN360 effects, or a source of patient-level evidence.

Use its [CSV files](../../data/synthetic/README.md) and [TFLs](../../tfl/README.md) to explain the shape of a database and output. Keep the invented study name visible when switching from the public case. No students need to run the generation scripts.

## Separate fictional diagnostic product

**CardioLab Connect** is an invented laboratory Lp(a) blood test with a portal delivering numeric results to healthcare professionals. Its illustrative intended use is measurement to support a clinician's cardiovascular risk assessment; it neither selects SLN360 therapy nor claims independently to predict an individual's future events. Specify population, specimen, setting, units, and limitations before proposing product claims.

The assay and connected portal are a teaching context for performance evidence, privacy, security, and data access. The portal's function does not automatically establish a device class or a particular Data Act obligation. It is not a Silence Therapeutics product, a SLN360 companion diagnostic, or a real marketed device.
